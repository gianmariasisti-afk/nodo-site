Privacy notice

For the nodo waitlist on getnodo.eu. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who is responsible

nodo is run by Gianmaria Sisti, Brussels, who decides how your data is used. Write to hello@getnodo.eu for anything in this notice.

What is collected

  • Your email address.
  • The phone you picked: iPhone, Android or both.
  • That you gave consent, and when you signed up.
  • If you arrived through a tagged link, the campaign name in it (for example "linkedin").

Nothing else. The site sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or trackers.

Visit statistics

To see how many people visit and where they come from, the site records each page view and waitlist sign-up as an anonymous count: the page, the website that linked to it (domain only), any campaign tags in the link, whether the screen is phone- or desktop-sized, and the browser language. It stores no IP address, no device or browser details and no identifier, so a visit cannot be linked to you or to your other visits, and nothing is saved on your device. These counts are kept in the same Supabase database. The legal basis is nodo's legitimate interest in knowing whether the site reaches its audience (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, nothing is recorded.

Why, and on what basis

To email you when nodo launches on the App Store or Google Play, and to send the occasional update before then. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, which stops all emails.

Where it is stored

In a database hosted by Supabase in its EU region (Ireland), which processes it on nodo's behalf. The site itself is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you load a page.

How long it is kept

Until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, and no longer than 12 months after nodo launches in the stores.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to receive a copy of it, by writing to hello@getnodo.eu. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

Sharing

Your address is never sold or shared for marketing. It is used only to write to you about nodo.
